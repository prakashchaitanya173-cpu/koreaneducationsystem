Historical Context

The "Education Miracle" on the Han River

In 1953, following the Korean War, South Korea's per-capita GDP was below $100 — poorer than most nations in sub-Saharan Africa. The country lacked natural resources, oil, or abundant minerals. Korean parents and the government made a unanimous decision: human talent would be the nation's only renewable resource.

1950s – 1970s

Universal Primary & Middle School

Rapid eradication of illiteracy through the Hangul alphabet; 100% elementary school enrollment achieved by 1965 despite severe budget scarcity.

1980s – 2000s

Expansion of Higher Education

Government-led founding of science universities (KAIST, POSTECH). Universal high school completion and explosion in female collegiate participation.

2020s & Beyond

AI-Powered Personalized Learning

Ministry of Education launches nationwide rollout of AI digital textbooks, coding from grade 3, and high school credit systems (고교학점제).
