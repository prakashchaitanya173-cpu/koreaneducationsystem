🇰🇷 Full Government Scholarship

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS / 대한민국 정부초청 장학생)

The Korean Government invites thousands of international undergraduate and graduate scholars annually through the NIIED (National Institute for International Education) with 100% full-ride funding:

✈️
Airfare & Relocation Round-trip international flight tickets and initial resettlement settlement grant.
🎓
100% Tuition Waived Full tuition covered for the entire 4-year degree (Bachelor's) or 2-3 years (Master's/PhD).
💵
Monthly Living Stipend Generous monthly living allowance (approx. 1,000,000 KRW/month) plus medical insurance.
🗣️
1-Year Language Course Pre-degree full-time intensive Korean language immersion training program included.
