School Life in South Korea

What is it really like to be a Korean student? From crisp school uniforms and homeroom bonds to afternoon cleaning duties and vibrant Dong-ari clubs.

Camaraderie & Respect

The Korean Classroom: A Tightly-Knit Family

In South Korea, classmates stay in the same home classroom for the entire academic year while subject teachers rotate. Under the compassionate guidance of their homeroom teacher (담임선생님), students form deep lifelong bonds, eat lunch together, and share daily chores.

Section C • High Stakes Assessment

The Suneung (수능) & College Admissions

Held every November, the College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT / 수능) is Korea's 1-day academic marathon where an entire nation pauses to honor student effort.

When an Entire Nation Pauses for Students

The Suneung test day is a rare cultural phenomenon. To guarantee zero noise during the 35-minute English listening comprehension exam, commercial flights across Korea cannot take off or land. Government offices delay opening to clear highway congestion, and police escort delayed students to test sites.

How Korean Universities Admit Students

Admissions in South Korea operate across two main pathways: Susie (Rolling early admission based on high school transcript grades) and Jeongsi (Regular admission based primarily on national Suneung scores).
