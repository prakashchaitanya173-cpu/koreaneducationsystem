💡 Quick Revision Notes For This Quiz

  • • Flight Groundings: For 35 minutes during the Suneung English listening comprehension test, all commercial aircraft are barred from taking off or landing nationwide.
  • • Unified System: Korea adopts the 6-3-3-4 pattern (6 years elementary, 3 years middle, 3 years high school, 4 years university).
  • • Academic Year: South Korean schools begin their first semester on March 1st / 2nd, with the second semester in late August.
  • • Classroom Care: Students clean their own classrooms daily after 7th period as part of character education (인성 교육).
