💡 Comprehensive Revision Guide (All Topics Summary)

Review these key academic facts across all five portal sections before or after taking the 12-question challenge:

1. Structure & Governance

  • • 6-3-3-4 Ladder: 6 yrs Elementary + 3 yrs Middle (9 yrs compulsory) + 3 yrs High School (all 12 yrs tuition-free) + 4 yrs University.
  • • Academic Year: Begins March 2nd across 2 semesters; ends the following February.
  • • Teacher Rotation: Public teachers pass Imyong-gosi and rotate every 4–5 years for nationwide quality equity.
  • • 2025 High School Credit System: Students earn 192 credits via personalized elective subjects.

2. Suneung & Admissions

  • • 35-Minute Flight Ban: All flights grounded nationwide during Period 3 English listening (13:05–13:40).
  • • Susie vs. Jeongsi: ~78% Early Rolling (school records & portfolios) vs. ~22% Regular (Suneung scores).
  • • Grading: Korean/Math/Inquiry use 9-tier relative curve (Top 4% = Grade 1); English (≥90) & Korean History (≥40) use absolute grading.
  • • EBS Linkage: Free public EBSi lectures & textbooks indirectly link to ~50% of Suneung concepts.

3. School Life & Culture

  • • Silnae-hwa & Cleaning: Indoor slippers at entrance lockers plus daily student classroom cleaning (인성 교육).
  • • Free Hot Meals (급식): Dietitian-designed eco-friendly lunches provided free from Grade 1 to Grade 12.
  • • Free Semester System: Exam-free Grade 7 semester for career exploration, debates, and labs.
  • • Good-Luck Gifts: Sticky Yeot (엿) & rice cakes symbolize passing ("sticking"); seaweed soup is avoided!

4. Korea vs. India & Global Stats

  • • Structures: Korea's single-track 6-3-3-4 vs. India's transitioning NEP 2020 5+3+3+4 across multiple boards.
  • • Elite Leagues: Korea's SKY (Seoul National, Korea, Yonsei) + KAIST vs. India's IITs, IIMs, and AIIMS.
  • • OECD #1 Attainment: ~69.6% of Koreans aged 25–34 hold tertiary degrees; literacy exceeds 99%.
  • • Study Korea 300K: Over 200,000 international students study in Korea, aided by full-ride GKS scholarships.
