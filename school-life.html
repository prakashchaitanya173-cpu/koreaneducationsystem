Everyday Life & Culture in Korean Schools

Explore the vibrant reality of being a South Korean student: crisp Gyobok uniforms, the loving guidance of homeroom teachers (담임), communal cleaning duties, night study (야자), and after-school Hagwons.

Camaraderie & Respect

The Korean Classroom: A Tightly-Knit Family

In South Korea, students spend their entire school day together in one fixed homeroom while specialized teachers rotate. Led by their devoted homeroom teacher (담임선생님), classmates bond like siblings, eat communal hot lunches, and work side-by-side.

Typical Daily Schedule

A Day in the Life of a Korean High Schooler

From early morning arrival to late-night study halls, high schoolers in Korea follow an intense, structured routine geared toward college entrance.

07:40 – 08:30

Arrival & Morning Reading

Change into white indoor slippers (실내화), morning homeroom attendance check, morning self-study.

08:40 – 16:30

Regular Classes & Lunch

7 subject periods (50 mins each), nutritious hot lunch with Kimchi and soup, afternoon classroom cleaning.

17:00 – 21:00

Yaja (Night Self-Study)

High school silent evening study hall in school classrooms or designated study cubicles (Dokseosil).

21:30 – 23:00

Hagwon Academy Session

Intensive private coaching in Gangnam/Daechi-dong until the national 22:00/23:00 curfew.
